# Phase 1 MVP: batch-tracked yard inventory, sales and public catalog

## Problem Statement

Kirthik Kumar runs a family granite, memorial and construction stone yard in Tamil Nadu. Every purchase, sale, customer and yard placement is written in paper registers. Because slabs of the same Product and Size bought on different dates at different prices get stacked together, older stock gets buried, sits for a year, weathers, and is eventually sold at cost or a loss. Nobody can say at a glance how much stock is in the yard, what it is worth, which Batches are getting old, or what the real Margin on a Sale was once engraving (Stickering) and freight are counted. His parents, who also run the yard, cannot use anything technical. Customers and contractors phone to ask what is in stock because there is nothing to send them.

## Solution

A responsive web app, signed into with Google by a short list of Team Members, where every delivery is recorded as its own Batch that is never merged with older stock, every Sale is taken from a specific Batch with Landed Cost and Margin computed automatically, the yard is shown Slot by Slot with a Clamp between deliveries and colour-coded Ageing Bands, a dashboard shows stock, value, revenue, margin and Stale stock, and an unauthenticated Public Catalog lists what is available with a WhatsApp button. The parents use it from their phones in the yard; Kirthik uses it from a laptop and corrects mistakes.

## User Stories

### Signing in and access

1. As a Team Member, I want to sign in with my Google account in one tap, so that I never type a password in the yard.
2. As a Team Member, I want my session to persist across days, so that I am not signing in every morning.
3. As an Admin, I want to add a Google email with a Role to the Team Member list, so that my parents can sign in without me setting up passwords or sending invites.
4. As an Admin, I want to remove a Team Member, so that a person who leaves loses access on their next request.
5. As an Admin, I want a Google account that is not on the list to sign in successfully but see nothing, so that nobody can guess their way into the data.
6. As a Yard Operator, I want to see all stock, costs and Margins, so that I can quote a fair price without calling Kirthik.
7. As a Yard Operator, I want Corrections, Settings and Team Member management hidden from me, so that I cannot break anything by accident.
8. As anyone, I want the sign-in page to say that access is limited to approved team members, so that a wrong-account attempt is understood rather than reported as a bug.

### Recording inward stock

9. As an operator, I want to log a delivery with purchase date (defaulting to today), Supplier, Product, Variant, Size, quantity, Unit Purchase Price and optional Freight Cost, so that the lorry's contents are on record before it leaves.
10. As an operator, I want to pick a Supplier from a searchable list or add a new one inline, so that a new quarry does not block the entry.
11. As an operator, I want to pick a Product from a searchable list or add one inline with a Category and a short abbreviation suggested from its initials, so that a new material like Tandur Yellow can be logged immediately.
12. As an operator, I want to type a Variant name and be told when it already exists for that Product, so that I know the delivery becomes a new Batch and is not merged.
13. As an operator, I want Size fields (length ft, breadth ft, thickness mm) required for Granite and Tiles and absent for Memorial, so that Doom Stones are logged by count without fake numbers.
14. As an operator, I want the Slot suggested from the Size and Category (4 ft, 5 ft, Doom Stones, Custom) and changeable, so that the record matches where the lorry actually unloaded.
15. As an operator, I want to see the Batch Code that will be generated before I save, so that I can write it on the physical stack.
16. As an operator, I want the Batch Code generated by the system, so that two people logging deliveries at the same moment never get the same code.
17. As an operator, I want the form to refuse a future purchase date, zero quantity or negative price, so that a slip of the finger does not corrupt stock.
18. As an operator, I want a reminder on the form that Batches are never merged, so that my parents stop looking for a "add to existing stock" button.
19. As an Admin, I want Freight Cost spread across the pieces into a Landed Cost, so that Margin and inventory value reflect what the stone truly cost me.

### Yard view

20. As an operator, I want the yard shown as tabs per Slot (4 ft, 5 ft, Doom Stones, Custom), so that the screen matches the physical yard.
21. As an operator, I want Batches inside a Slot grouped by length × breadth, so that I can find the 4 × 2.5 pile without reading every row.
22. As an operator, I want each Batch shown as its own row with Product, Variant, Batch Code, Size, thickness, bought, sold and Available, so that no two deliveries are ever shown as one number.
23. As an operator, I want a Clamp line between consecutive Batches of the same Stock Line saying "New batch arrived N days later", so that the screen mirrors the physical divider in the yard.
24. As an operator, I want every Batch colour-coded by Ageing Band (Fresh green, Ageing amber, Stale red) with its Age in days, so that old stock is visible from across the room, including in sunlight on a phone.
25. As an operator, I want Batches oldest first by default with a Newest-first option, so that the stock to sell next is at the top.
26. As an operator, I want to search by product, variant or supplier text and filter by Product, Variant, Thickness, Supplier and Age (over 90, 180, 365 days), so that I can answer "how many 4 × 2 Black Pearl 16 mm do we have" in seconds.
27. As an operator, I want Sold Out Batches hidden by default with a toggle to show them, so that the yard view shows what is physically there but history is not lost.
28. As an operator, I want a "Sell from this Batch" action on each row that opens the Sell form with that Batch preselected, so that a sale from the yard is two taps.
29. As an operator, I want a collapsed summary per Slot ("3 batches, 42 available"), so that I can scan the whole yard on one screen.

### Selling stone

30. As an operator, I want to record a Sale with sale date (defaulting to today), Payment Mode (Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer), Customer, Stock Line, Batch, quantity and Sale Price per piece, so that the outward register is replaced.
31. As an operator, I want to search Customers by name or phone and add a new one inline with name, phone and Customer Type, so that a first-time contractor does not block the sale.
32. As an operator, I want typing a phone number that already exists to select that Customer instead of creating a duplicate, so that Murugan is one customer, not five.
33. As an operator, I want a shared Walk-in Customer for cash buyers who give no details, so that every Sale still has a Customer.
34. As an operator, I want to search Stock Lines (Product, Variant, Size, Available count) and see only lines with stock, so that I never pick something that is not in the yard.
35. As an operator, I want the FIFO Listing of Batches for that Stock Line, oldest first, each with Age, Ageing Band, purchase date and Available, so that the oldest stock is the obvious choice.
36. As an operator, I want to pick any listed Batch without being blocked, so that the record matches the pile the stone physically came from even when the older pile is damaged.
37. As an operator, I want the quantity refused if it exceeds the Batch's Available, so that stock never goes negative.
38. As an operator, I want the Batch's Landed Cost filled in automatically and shown read-only, so that I do not have to look up what we paid.
39. As an operator, I want a toggle to add Stickering with a per-piece Stickering Cost and Stickering Price, so that engraving for a temple or memorial is part of the same Sale.
40. As an operator, I want an optional Misc Expense that is recorded but kept out of Margin, so that transport to site is tracked without distorting profit.
41. As an operator, I want an order summary that updates live with stone total, Landed Cost, base margin, Stickering margin, Misc Expense, total Margin and Margin percentage, so that I can see the profit before I press Record Sale.

## Implementation Decisions

### Architecture

- Next.js App Router with TypeScript, Tailwind v4 and shadcn/ui, hosted on Vercel. Supabase (Mumbai region) provides Google-only Auth, Postgres, RLS and the public read path. Details in the tech stack doc.
- All business rules live in Postgres functions and views, called via Supabase RPC (ADR 0001). Server components read views; server actions validate input with zod and call functions. Screens hold no rules.
- No ORM. Schema is plain SQL migrations managed by the Supabase CLI; TypeScript types are generated from the database.
- Malleability principles from the Phase 1 decisions doc apply: raw facts stored, everything else derived; categorical columns are text with check constraints, not Postgres enums; single tenant; no premature scale work.

### Schema

- Tables: products (name, category, abbreviation unique), variants (product, name, unique per product), suppliers, customers (name, phone unique when present, customer_type), batches, sales, settings (single row), team_members (email, role).
- Batch stores: batch_code unique, variant, supplier, purchase_date, length_ft, breadth_ft, thickness_mm (all three null for Memorial, all present otherwise, enforced by a check), slot, initial_units, units_sold, unit_purchase_price, freight_cost, landed_cost as a generated column, notes, created_by, updated_by, timestamps. Available is derived as initial_units minus units_sold in views and functions; a check keeps units_sold between zero and initial_units.
- Sale stores: sale_date, batch, customer, quantity, sale_price (per piece), landed_cost snapshot, has_stickering, stickering_cost, stickering_price (per piece), misc_expense, payment_mode, created_by, updated_by, timestamps. Revenue, margin and margin percentage are computed in views from these raw fields, not stored.
- Settings row: ageing_after_days, stale_after_days (check ageing < stale), catalog_public, whatsapp_number, business name.
- Check constraints: purchase_date and sale_date not in the future; quantities positive; prices non-negative; slot, customer_type, payment_mode, role and category limited to the agreed value lists.
- Money is numeric(12,2); percentages numeric(5,2); dates are date columns interpreted in IST.

### Functions (the write API)

- create_batch: validates, derives default slot when none given, generates the Batch Code inside the transaction with a per-product-per-day sequence, inserts, returns the row.
- record_sale: locks the Batch row, refuses if quantity exceeds Available, snapshots landed_cost, increments units_sold, inserts the Sale. Concurrent oversell fails with a clear error.
- correct_batch and delete_batch: Admin only; delete refused when Sales exist; quantity edits refused when Available would go negative.
- correct_sale and delete_sale: Admin only; rebalances units_sold on the old and new Batch inside one transaction and re-snapshots landed_cost when the Batch changes.
- All functions are security definer with an internal check of the caller's Team Member row and Role; RLS still applies to direct reads.

### Views (the read API)

- v_yard_batches: every Batch with Available, Age in days, Ageing Band from settings, Sold Out flag, and the gap in days to the previous Batch of the same Stock Line for the Clamp label.
- v_stock_lines: grouped by Product, Variant and Size with total Available and Batch count. Used by the Sell form combobox, the Command K palette and the Public Catalog.
- v_public_catalog: subset of v_stock_lines with Available above zero and only public columns; the only object granted to the anon role, and it returns no rows when catalog_public is false.
- v_sales: Sales joined with derived totals, margin and margin percentage.
- Dashboard queries as views or functions for the KPI definitions in the decisions doc, including active stock at a given date for the month-over-month comparison.
- v_customers with last purchase date derived from Sales.

### Access

- RLS on every table: a row is visible or writable only when the JWT email matches a Team Member. Admin-only operations are enforced inside the functions.
- First Admin seeded by migration with a placeholder email to be replaced before deploy. Invite is a plain insert into team_members; no email is sent.
- The Public Catalog route is unauthenticated and reads v_public_catalog server-side with the anon key; the route returns 404 when the catalog is switched off.

### UI

- Design tokens (Geist, JetBrains Mono, orange primary, light and dark palettes, radii) copied from the Pencil file into global CSS variables. shadcn components map one to one to the Pencil library.
- Forms use react-hook-form with zod schemas shared with the server actions.
- Charts via Recharts through the shadcn chart wrapper: one donut, one horizontal bar.
- Breakpoint at 768 px switches sidebar to tab bar. A manifest and icons provide the home-screen install; no service worker.
- Command K palette present on desktop only.

## Testing Decisions

- A good test drives the system through a seam a real caller uses and asserts on observable results: rows returned, errors raised, page content rendered. It does not inspect internal columns that are not part of the contract, mock the database, or re-derive the formula it is checking.
- Seam 1 (primary): the Supabase RPC and view boundary, exercised with Vitest and supabase-js against a local Supabase instance, signed in as an Admin, a Yard Operator and anonymous. Covers: Batch creation and Batch Code uniqueness under concurrent inserts; Landed Cost with and without freight; record_sale refusing oversell and rejecting concurrent overs; landed_cost snapshot immune to later Batch edits; Margin and percentage arithmetic; FIFO Listing order and Ageing Bands at the thresholds; Sold Out and Clamp gaps; Corrections rebalancing and refusals; RLS denying non-members and anon everything except v_public_catalog; catalog hidden when switched off; dashboard KPIs against a small seeded dataset with known answers.
- Seam 2 (thin): Playwright against the Next app and local Supabase for three flows only: log a delivery and see it in the yard; sell from a Batch and see Available drop and Margin shown; open the Public Catalog and see the line with the right count and no price. These prove wiring, not rules.
- No prior art exists; the repo is a fresh scaffold. These two suites establish the convention.
- Migrations are applied fresh in CI before each suite; seed data lives in a test fixture, not in production migrations, except the Walk-in Customer, the settings row and the first Admin.

## Out of Scope

- Credit sales, dues and part payments
- Sales with multiple Batches or receipt grouping
- Photos in the Public Catalog
- Merge tools for duplicate Products, Variants, Suppliers or Customers
- Audit log beyond updated_by and updated_at
- Offline mode or service worker
- Hiding costs and Margins from Yard Operators
- Invoicing, GST, printing
- AI query assistant, festival season comparison, WhatsApp Business API, Tiles and Electricals franchise lines (Phase 2)
- Multi-tenancy or a tenant slug in the catalog URL
