# Granite Inventory

Yard inventory and sales tracking for a granite, memorial stone and construction stone supply business. Stock is tracked per purchase batch, never pooled, so old stock is always visible and sold first. Inputs rejected by constraints reachable through the UI or documented API raise a readable business-rule message before the database constraint fires, rather than exposing raw Postgres text.

## Language

### Stock

**Product**:
A stone material, such as Black Pearl, Jet Black, G20 or Doom Stone. Belongs to a Category. May have one image, set by an Admin in Settings, shown on every size of it in the Public Catalog and its preview; without one the catalog shows a colour swatch.
_Avoid_: Material, stone type, item

**Variant**:
A grade, finish or quality of a Product, such as Grade 1 or Mirror Polish. A Variant has no dimensions; dimensions belong to the Batch. Hidden from every screen: new Batches get the Product's "Standard" Variant, and older Variants stay stored but are never shown or used for grouping.
_Avoid_: Quality, SKU, grade (as a noun)

**Batch**:
A single purchase of one Product in one size, received on one date from one Supplier. Batches are never merged, even when product and size match an existing Batch.
_Avoid_: Lot, stock line, purchase

**Stock Line**:
All Batches sharing one Product and Size, seen as a single sellable item. What the Sell form searches and what the Public Catalog lists. Not stored; derived from Batches.
_Avoid_: SKU, item, product line

**Batch Code**:
The human-readable identifier of a Batch, formed from the Product's abbreviation, the purchase date and a per-day sequence, such as BP-07SEP26-01. Generated by the system, never typed. Shown on screens as "Purchase code", so it is never confused with a Stack's Batch number. Purchase dates run from 2000-01-01 through the current IST business date so the two-digit year remains unambiguous.
_Avoid_: Batch ID, lot number

**Unit Purchase Price**:
What a Batch cost per square foot of Area, freight included; for Memorial, which has no Size, per piece. The person logging the Batch adds the freight into this price before entering it.

**Freight Cost**:
A legacy stored fact: lorry and handling cost for a whole Batch, no longer entered anywhere. Batches saved before freight moved into the Unit Purchase Price had it folded in, so every Batch now holds 0.
_Avoid_: Misc cost, inward cost

**Landed Cost**:
The true per-piece cost of a Batch. It equals the Area times the Unit Purchase Price, or the Unit Purchase Price alone for Memorial (plus any legacy Freight Cost spread evenly over the pieces bought). All margins and inventory value use Landed Cost.
_Avoid_: Purchase price (when freight matters), cost price

**Total cost**:
What the whole Batch cost: pieces bought times Landed Cost, so Area times pieces times Unit Purchase Price (pieces times price for Memorial); any legacy Freight Cost is already inside Landed Cost. A Batch of 15 pieces of 4 × 2 ft at ₹181.25 per sq ft has a Total cost of ₹21,750. Derived in `v_batches` and shown in Recent batches, for archived Batches too.

**Area**:
Length times breadth of one piece, in square feet. Shown read-only beside Length and Breadth on the Inward form and the Edit dialog; not stored.

**Category**:
The broad line a Product belongs to: Granite, Memorial, Tiles.

**Available**:
Pieces of a Batch still in the yard: pieces bought minus pieces sold. Never below zero.
_Avoid_: Present, remaining, on hand, current quantity

**Clamp**:
The visual separator drawn between two consecutive Batches of the same Stock Line in the Yard view, mirroring the physical divider placed between deliveries.
_Avoid_: Separator, divider

**Sold Out**:
A Batch whose Available is zero. Hidden from the Yard view by default, kept for history.
_Avoid_: Closed, empty

**Archived**:
A Batch, Product or Supplier an Admin has set aside without deleting it. An Archived Batch leaves Sell, Stock Lines, the Public Catalog and the stock KPIs, and the Yard shows it only with "Show archived"; its Sales stay in history. Only an Archived Batch with no Sales can be deleted. An Archived Product or Supplier leaves the Inward pickers and takes no new Batches, while existing Batches keep it. Unarchiving brings each back.
_Avoid_: Hidden, disabled, soft-deleted

**Age**:
Days since a Batch's purchase date. Measured from purchase, not from last Sale.

**Ageing Band**:
Fresh, Ageing or Stale, decided by a Batch's Age against two thresholds set by the Admin (Ageing after N days, Stale after M days, N < M). Stale Batches are flagged for discount or promotion.
_Avoid_: Old stock, status (for age)

**Correction**:
An Admin editing, archiving or deleting a Batch or Sale after the fact. Available is re-balanced and a Correction is refused if it would make Available negative.
_Avoid_: Reversal, adjustment

### Selling

**Sale**:
A quantity of pieces taken from exactly one Batch by one Customer on one date. The Sale date cannot be before that Batch's purchase date. A customer buying from two Batches produces two Sales. A Sale may belong to a Bill; Sales recorded before Bills existed belong to none.
_Avoid_: Order, invoice, outward entry, transaction

**Bill**:
The Sales recorded together in one purchase by one Customer on one date, paid through its Payment Split. A Bill holds no money of its own: its totals are the sum of its Sales, and each Sale still takes from exactly one Batch. Created only by `record_bill`; a Bill with no Sales left is removed. An Admin can delete a whole Bill at once: all its Sales go and their pieces return to their Batches. A Sale on a Bill keeps the Bill's Customer and Sale date: correcting the Sale cannot change them. How it was paid lives on the Bill, not on its Sales.
_Avoid_: Invoice, order, receipt, cart (the cart is the unsaved screen state before a Bill exists)

**FIFO Listing**:
When selling, matching Batches are shown oldest first with their Ageing Band. The operator picks the Batch the stone physically came from; nothing is blocked.
_Avoid_: FIFO enforcement

**Sale Rate**:
What the Customer pays per square foot of Area for the stone alone, excluding Stickering; for Memorial, which has no Size, per piece. It is the figure typed on Sell Stone and Edit sale, under the label "Stone Sale Price (₹)" with the hint "Per sq ft" or "Per piece". A sale of 10 pieces of 4 × 2 ft at ₹250 per sq ft is ₹20,000.
_Avoid_: Selling rate, price per sq ft (as the canonical term)

**Sale Price**:
What the Customer pays per piece for the stone alone, excluding Stickering. Derived, never typed: the Area of the piece at the time of the Sale times the Sale Rate (the Sale Rate itself for Memorial), rounded to paise. Margin, revenue and every dashboard figure read it.
_Avoid_: Rate, selling rate

**Stickering**:
Engraving of names, scripture or donor details onto stone, sold as an add-on to a Sale and priced like the stone: Area times a rate times the pieces. Has a Stickering Cost (paid to the engraver) and a Stickering Price (charged to the Customer), each worked out per piece as the Area of the piece times its rate (the rate itself for Memorial), rounded to paise. 4 pieces of 5 × 3 ft at ₹60 per sq ft is a Stickering Price of ₹3,600, and at ₹40 per sq ft a Stickering Cost of ₹2,400.
_Avoid_: Lettering, engraving service (as the canonical term)

**Stickering Rate**:
What the Customer pays per square foot of Area for Stickering; for Memorial, which has no Size, per piece. It is the figure typed on Sell Stone and Edit sale, under the label "Stickering Price (₹)" with the hint "Per sq ft, charged to the customer" or "Per piece, charged to the customer". A Sale stores it as typed; the per piece Stickering Price is derived from it.
_Avoid_: Stickering price per sq ft (as the canonical term)

**Stickering Cost Rate**:
What the engraver is paid per square foot of Area; for Memorial, per piece. It is typed under the label "Stickering Cost (₹)" with the hint "Per sq ft, paid to the engraver" or "Per piece, paid to the engraver". The per piece Stickering Cost is derived from it.
_Avoid_: Engraver rate

**Payment Mode**:
How money came in: Cash, UPI or Bank Transfer. A Bill may be paid in more than one (see Payment Split); a Sale recorded without a Bill has exactly one. Every Sale is treated as paid in full; credit and dues are out of scope.

**Money received**:
The payment amounts that came in per Payment Mode over a period, summed from each Bill's Payment Split and from Sales without a Bill. It counts what the Customer paid, not Margin, and a payment falls in the period of its Sale date. Shown on Overview in the Money received card.
_Avoid_: Collected (the Margin Table's word for the same sum over its own range), Revenue

**Payment Split**:
How one Bill was paid: an amount per Payment Mode, each mode at most once, for example ₹30,000 Cash + ₹20,000 UPI on a ₹50,000 Bill. The amounts always add up exactly to what the Customer pays for the Bill (Stone Sale Price plus Stickering Price over its Sales). A Bill paid in one mode has one payment of its whole total, which follows the total when a Sale is corrected or deleted. A mixed Bill's Margin is shared across its payments in proportion to amount.
_Avoid_: Part payment, partial payment (nothing is ever left unpaid)

**Customer**:
The person or organisation a Sale is made to. Every Sale has a Customer; anonymous cash buyers go to a shared Walk-in Customer.
The Walk-in Customer flag is immutable for every role.
_Avoid_: Client, buyer, party

**Customer Type**:
One of Regular, Contractor, Engineer, Trust or Retail. Trust covers temples, churches and charitable trusts. Retail is shown as "Walk-in" in the UI.
_Avoid_: Tier, segment

**Supplier**:
The quarry or dealer a Batch was bought from. Created on the fly from the inward form.
_Avoid_: Seller, vendor

### Public

**Public Catalog**:
The unauthenticated page listing every Stock Line with Available above zero: product, size and exact count. Never shows prices, costs, suppliers, batches or dates. Can be switched off by the Admin.
_Avoid_: Public view, stock view, storefront

**Inquiry**:
A visitor tapping "Call to Inquire" on the Public Catalog, which opens WhatsApp to the business number with the Stock Line prefilled. Nothing is recorded.
_Avoid_: Reservation, booking, lead

### People

**Team Member**:
A Google account allowed to sign in, with a Role. Nobody outside the Team Member list can see anything but the Public Catalog.
_Avoid_: User, staff, account

**Role**:
Admin or Yard Operator. Both see all stock, costs and margins; only Admin makes Corrections, changes Settings and manages Team Members.

**Margin**:
Per Sale: (Sale Price − Landed Cost) × quantity, where the Sale Price is Area × Sale Rate, plus (Stickering Price − Stickering Cost) × quantity, where the Stickering Price is Area × Stickering Rate and the Stickering Cost is Area × Stickering Cost Rate. Margin percentage is Margin divided by total billed (stone plus Stickering).
_Avoid_: Profit, markup

**Size**:
Length and breadth in feet plus thickness in millimetres of every piece in a Batch. Required for Granite and Tiles; absent for Memorial, which is tracked by unit count.
_Avoid_: Dimension (alone), spec

**Slot**:
The physical area of the yard where a Batch is stacked: 4 ft Slot, 5 ft Slot, Doom Stones, Custom, or any name the operator types (up to 20 characters). Suggested from the Batch's Size and Category, but the operator has the final say.
_Avoid_: Location, zone, rack

**Stack**:
One physical pile of stone inside a Slot, named by the number written on it, such as 4 ft Slot · 12. Shown to users as "Batch number", the client's word. Several Batches can go onto the same Stack; a Slot plus a number names one Stack, so 4 ft Slot · 12 and 5 ft Slot · 12 are different. Every new Batch names its Stack; Batches logged before Stacks existed have none. Stored as a fact on the Batch, not as its own record.
_Avoid_: Pile, lot, Batch (for the pile)

**Doom Stone**:
A Memorial Product: headstones and crosses in standard shapes, tracked by unit count with no Size.
_Avoid_: Memorial stone, grave stone

### Chat

**Chat**:
The Ask panel on Overview. A Team Member types a question in plain words and a model answers with text and charts, reading only the chat views through a read-only role. It can never change data.
_Avoid_: Assistant, bot, AI

**Customer alias**:
How a Customer appears to the model: `C-` followed by the first six characters of the Customer's id, such as C-3f9a2b. Names and phones never reach the model; the browser swaps an alias back to the name for display.
_Avoid_: Customer code, pseudonym
