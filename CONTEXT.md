# Granite Inventory

Yard inventory and sales tracking for a granite, memorial stone and construction stone supply business. Stock is tracked per purchase batch, never pooled, so old stock is always visible and sold first.

## Language

### Stock

**Product**:
A stone material, such as Black Pearl, Jet Black, G20 or Doom Stone. Belongs to a Category.
_Avoid_: Material, stone type, item

**Variant**:
A grade, finish or quality of a Product, such as Grade 1 or Mirror Polish. A Variant has no dimensions; dimensions belong to the Batch.
_Avoid_: Quality, SKU, grade (as a noun)

**Batch**:
A single purchase of one Variant in one size, received on one date from one Supplier. Batches are never merged, even when product, variant and size match an existing Batch.
_Avoid_: Lot, stock line, purchase

**Stock Line**:
All Batches sharing one Product, Variant and Size, seen as a single sellable item. What the Sell form searches and what the Public Catalog lists. Not stored; derived from Batches.
_Avoid_: SKU, item, product line

**Batch Code**:
The human-readable identifier of a Batch, formed from the Product's abbreviation, the purchase date and a per-day sequence, such as BP-07SEP26-01. Generated by the system, never typed.
_Avoid_: Batch ID, lot number

**Unit Purchase Price**:
What the Supplier charged per piece for a Batch, before freight.

**Freight Cost**:
Lorry and handling cost paid to bring a whole Batch to the yard. Optional.
_Avoid_: Misc cost, inward cost

**Landed Cost**:
The true per-piece cost of a Batch: Unit Purchase Price plus Freight Cost spread evenly over the pieces bought. All margins and inventory value use Landed Cost.
_Avoid_: Purchase price (when freight matters), cost price

**Category**:
The broad line a Product belongs to: Granite, Memorial, Tiles.

**Available**:
Pieces of a Batch still in the yard: pieces bought minus pieces sold. Never below zero.
_Avoid_: Present, remaining, on hand, current quantity

**Clamp**:
The visual separator drawn between two consecutive Batches of the same Stock Line in the Yard view, mirroring the physical divider placed between deliveries.
_Avoid_: Separator, divider

**Sold Out**:
A Batch whose Available is zero. Hidden from the Yard view by default, kept for history.
_Avoid_: Closed, archived, empty

**Age**:
Days since a Batch's purchase date. Measured from purchase, not from last Sale.

**Ageing Band**:
Fresh, Ageing or Stale, decided by a Batch's Age against two thresholds set by the Admin (Ageing after N days, Stale after M days, N < M). Stale Batches are flagged for discount or promotion.
_Avoid_: Old stock, status (for age)

**Correction**:
An Admin editing or deleting a Batch or Sale after the fact. Available is re-balanced and a Correction is refused if it would make Available negative.
_Avoid_: Reversal, adjustment

### Selling

**Sale**:
A quantity of pieces taken from exactly one Batch by one Customer on one date. A customer buying from two Batches produces two Sales.
_Avoid_: Order, invoice, outward entry, transaction

**FIFO Listing**:
When selling, matching Batches are shown oldest first with their Ageing Band. The operator picks the Batch the stone physically came from; nothing is blocked.
_Avoid_: FIFO enforcement

**Sale Price**:
What the Customer pays per piece for the stone alone, excluding Stickering.
_Avoid_: Rate, selling rate

**Stickering**:
Engraving of names, scripture or donor details onto stone, sold as an add-on to a Sale. Has a per-piece Stickering Cost (paid to the engraver) and a per-piece Stickering Price (charged to the Customer).
_Avoid_: Lettering, engraving service (as the canonical term)

**Payment Mode**:
How a Sale was paid: Cash, UPI or Bank Transfer. Every Sale is treated as paid in full; credit and dues are out of scope.

**Misc Expense**:
A one-off cost on a Sale such as transport to site or loading. Recorded for bookkeeping but excluded from Margin.

**Customer**:
The person or organisation a Sale is made to. Every Sale has a Customer; anonymous cash buyers go to a shared Walk-in Customer.
_Avoid_: Client, buyer, party

**Customer Type**:
One of Regular, Contractor, Engineer, Trust or Retail. Trust covers temples, churches and charitable trusts. Retail is shown as "Walk-in" in the UI.
_Avoid_: Tier, segment

**Supplier**:
The quarry or dealer a Batch was bought from. Created on the fly from the inward form.
_Avoid_: Seller, vendor

### Public

**Public Catalog**:
The unauthenticated page listing every Stock Line with Available above zero: product, variant, size and exact count. Never shows prices, costs, suppliers, batches or dates. Can be switched off by the Admin.
_Avoid_: Public view, stock view, storefront

**Inquiry**:
A visitor tapping "Call to Inquire" on the Public Catalog, which opens WhatsApp to the business number with the Stock Line prefilled. Nothing is recorded.
_Avoid_: Reservation, booking, lead

### People

**Team Member**:
A Google account allowed to sign in, with a Role. Nobody outside the Team Member list can see anything but the Public Catalog.
_Avoid_: User, staff, account

**Role**:
Admin or Yard Operator. Both see all stock, costs and margins; only Admin makes Corrections, changes Settings and manages Team Members.

**Margin**:
Per Sale: (Sale Price − Landed Cost) × quantity, plus (Stickering Price − Stickering Cost) × quantity. Margin percentage is Margin divided by total billed (stone plus Stickering).
_Avoid_: Profit, markup

**Size**:
Length and breadth in feet plus thickness in millimetres of every piece in a Batch. Required for Granite and Tiles; absent for Memorial, whose Variant name already carries the shape.
_Avoid_: Dimension (alone), spec

**Slot**:
The physical area of the yard where a Batch is stacked: 4 ft Slot, 5 ft Slot, Doom Stones or Custom. Suggested from the Batch's Size and Category, but the operator has the final say.
_Avoid_: Location, zone, rack

**Doom Stone**:
A Memorial Product: headstones and crosses in standard shapes, tracked by unit count with no Size.
_Avoid_: Memorial stone, grave stone
